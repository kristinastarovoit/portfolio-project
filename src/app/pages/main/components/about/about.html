About me

My journey into web development started during my training as a media designer, where I first worked with HTML and CSS. Driven by curiosity and the constant digital evolution, I wanted to understand not only how websites look, but why they work the way they do. Asking "Why?" and "How?" has always been part of the way I learn. What excites me most about coding is solving problems. I enjoy diving deep into challenges and staying persistent until I find a solution. Every project teaches me something new, and that's exactly what motivates me—the opportunity to continuously learn, improve, and grow as a developer.
